# 🏫 BEESKUL

**BEESKUL** is a multi-tenant SaaS platform designed for **primary and secondary schools**.

I founded and built BEESKUL to help schools manage their academic, administrative, financial, and communication operations from a centralized platform.

### ⚡ Core Capabilities

* 👨‍🎓 Student Management
* 👩‍🏫 Teacher & Staff Management
* 📚 Academic Management
* 📝 Examination & Result Management
* 📊 Broadsheets & Academic Reports
* 🕐 Timetable Management
* 📅 Attendance Management
* 💰 School Fee Management
* 💳 Online Payments
* 🏦 Payment Gateway Integration
* 📱 Parent Communication
* 🔐 Secure Result Access
* 📈 School Financial Management
* 🧾 Invoices & Billing
* 🔑 Role-Based Access Control
* 🏢 Multi-Tenant Architecture
* 🌐 Dedicated School Subdomains

### 🚀 SaaS Infrastructure

BEESKUL has evolved from a school management application into a **production SaaS platform**.

The platform includes infrastructure for:

* Self-service school registration
* Automatic tenant provisioning
* Multi-tenant school architecture
* Subscription management
* Payment processing
* School-specific domains
* Automated communication
* Secure result access
* Financial management
* Role-based permissions

BEESKUL is a **real production product** that I actively build, deploy, maintain, and improve based on the requirements of real schools.
