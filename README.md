## 🏫 BEESKUL

**BEESKUL** is a multi-tenant SaaS Student Management System designed for schools.

I founded and built BEESKUL to help schools manage their daily academic and administrative operations from a single platform.

### ⚡ What BEESKUL Provides

* 👨‍🎓 Student Management
* 👩‍🏫 Teacher & Staff Management
* 📚 Academic Management
* 📝 Examination & Result Management
* 📊 Broadsheets & Academic Reports
* 🕐 Timetable Management
* 📅 Attendance Management
* 💰 School Fee & Payment Management
* 📱 Parent Communication
* 🔐 Student Result PIN System
* 💳 Online Payments
* 🏦 Payment Gateway Integration
* 🏢 Multi-Tenant School Architecture
* 🔑 Role-Based Access Control
* 📈 School Financial Management
* 🌐 Dedicated School Subdomains
* 🧾 Invoices & Subscription Management

### 🚀 SaaS Features

BEESKUL is continuously evolving toward a more automated and self-service SaaS platform.

Current development includes:

* 🆕 **Self-service school registration**
* ⚡ Schools can sign up and create their accounts without manual setup
* 🏫 Automatic tenant/school provisioning
* 💳 **Paystack payment integration**
* 🏦 **Moniepoint payment integration**
* 🔐 **Student PINs for secure result checking**
* 📊 Subscription and billing management
* 🔒 Subscription access control
* 📱 Automated communication
* ⚙️ Automated school configuration
* 🚀 Continuous performance and scalability improvements

> BEESKUL is not just a project I am learning from. It is a real product that I actively build, maintain, deploy, and improve.
